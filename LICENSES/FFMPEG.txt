FFmpeg — LGPL 2.1+ attribution
===============================

This product incorporates software from the FFmpeg project
(https://ffmpeg.org). FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License (LGPL), version 2.1 or later.

The specific FFmpeg build bundled with unIO is the "LGPL" variant
of the FFmpeg-Builds project by BtbN
(https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds). This variant intentionally
excludes GPL-only components such as libx264, libx265, and
libfdk-aac. The H.264 software encoder included (libopenh264) is
provided by Cisco Systems under the BSD license; Cisco also covers
the H.264 patent royalties for end users under terms published on
their OpenH264 website.

Hardware H.264 encoders (NVIDIA NVENC, Intel Quick Sync, AMD AMF,
VA-API, Apple VideoToolbox) are accessed through FFmpeg wrappers
over vendor SDKs. The hardware vendors have already licensed H.264
patents for their encoders, so end-users of this app incur no
additional royalty obligations when the hardware path is used.

To obtain the corresponding source code for the bundled FFmpeg
binary, fetch the release matching the version string reported by
``ffmpeg -version`` from:

    https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
    https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
